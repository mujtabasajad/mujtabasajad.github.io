# 100 days of Inference Engineering
I've been looking for opportunities to learn about what happens with AI inference systems, I have found countless blogs about how to deploy a model etc but I think it is more interesting to understand how to make a deployment faster, how to look for bottlenecks, where do they even come from, how to fully utilise all your resources and lots of other things!

So for the next 100 days I am going to spend every day learning something new about this. I want to be able to understand the fundamentals of GPU computing all the way up to LLM inference.

## What I will be learning 
I will be working through a lot of stuff and will hopefully cover:
     - CUDA programming
     - GPU Profiling
     - Kernel Optimisation
     - KV Caching
     - PagedAttention
     - Memory management
     - Quantisation
     - Speculative decoding
     - Multi GPU Inference
     - vLLM
     - TensorRT-LLM
     - Triton
     - Nsight
     - GPU Scheduling
     - RunAI
     - A LOT MORE

I do not have a clue how I will cover it each day but I will start by working through the Inference Engineering book and see what happens from there.
